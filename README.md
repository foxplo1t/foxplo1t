# Hi, I'm Giannis 🦊

**Computer, Informatics & Telecommunications Engineering student** at the [International Hellenic University](https://www.ict.ihu.gr/) (Serres, Greece).

I like building things end to end: from a calculator that talks to an AI, to a live website students use to study, to breaking challenges in CTF competitions. My interests sit where **software, AI, hardware and security** meet.

## 🚀 Projects

### 🧮 [CalcAI](https://github.com/foxplo1t/ai-calculator): an AI-powered calculator on a Raspberry Pi
A real pocket calculator on the outside, a Claude AI assistant on the inside. Boots straight into a working calculator; a key combo switches to an AI chat with streaming replies, photo questions from a built-in camera, an on-screen keyboard driven by the keypad, Wi-Fi setup and battery management.
`Python` `Raspberry Pi` `Claude API` `Pillow` `Linux / systemd`

### 🎮 [Study Quest](https://github.com/foxplo1t/gamified-study): a website that makes studying feel like a game
Live at **[intzirtzis.xyz](https://intzirtzis.xyz)**. Pixel-art battles, daily quests and spaced repetition turn 5 computer-engineering courses (576 questions, 43 lessons) into an adventure. Accounts, leaderboard, character creator, and a security-first backend.
`JavaScript` `HTML5 Canvas` `PHP 8` `SQLite` `KaTeX` `Security`

### 🏁 [CTF write-ups](https://github.com/foxplo1t/ctf-writeups)
Detailed solutions from CTF competitions with team **IHU_Serres**: forensics, reverse engineering, OSINT and more.
**cruXipher 2026:** 8 challenges solved, 650 pts.
